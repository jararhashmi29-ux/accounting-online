How double-entry works

Every journal entry must have equal total Debit and Credit. The software checks this before saving.

Opening balances are entered as a balanced journal entry, usually against “Opening Balance Equity”. Keep a backup copy regularly.

Chart of Accounts

New Journal Entry

Enter any bank, cash, vendor, customer, expense, income or other account on each line. Use Debit and Credit as required; with two lines, entering an amount on one side automatically fills the opposite side on the other line. Selecting an account automatically fills its name in Line Description; you can edit or clear it. Use × Remove to remove a line. Total Debit must equal total Credit. Add a Party name to connect the entry to a vendor/customer ledger.
